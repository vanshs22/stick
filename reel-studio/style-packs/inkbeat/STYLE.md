# Inkbeat (3d)

Toon-shaded 3D cartoon Shorts with a camera that is always moving. Painted textures, ink outlines, saturated light, one character family, key facts painted on props. Based on the MIT-licensed inkbeat-shorts skill (renderers/inkbeat, notices kept).

Best for: explainers and stories told in one or two dressed places (a room, a street) where every fact can be an object.

Use: `skills/inkbeat/SKILL.md`. Storyboard = `ink-plan.json` -> `node tools/ink/plan.mjs` -> `node tools/ink/stills.mjs`. Example: `examples/showcase-bughunter-ink/`.
