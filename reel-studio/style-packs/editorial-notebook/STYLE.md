# Editorial notebook (2d)

Calm cream paper, italic serif accent word, windows and terminals that swing in. Explains one idea simply.

Best for: software and idea explainers, tutorials, how-to.

## Rules
- One accent colour (orange) used for the single most important word or number per shot.
- Headline words rise out of a mask; the accent word is italic serif.
- Windows and phones swing in from the right with a small 3D turn; everything exits before the next shot starts.
- Real screenshots beat drawn UI: use ProofCard or BrowserWindow with a capture whenever a real thing is named.
- Motion is calm: springs with little overshoot, no shake, no flashes.

## Defaults
Captions `headline`, shots 1.5-3.5 s, music `upbeat`.
Camera: No camera: layout and motion only. Slow 2 percent push on cards.
Type: Heavy sans headlines, italic serif accent, mono labels.
Transitions: cut, slide, mask, swing
Sound: upbeat bed, soft pop on each card, tick on steps, type on terminals, ding on the comment box

## Components
TitleCard, BigStat, NotebookPage, Terminal, BrowserWindow, PhoneFrame, ChatBubbles, CommentBox, Checklist, Highlight, KineticList, ProofCard, BarChart, LineChart, Versus

## Pitfalls
- More than one accent word per shot.
- Text smaller than 48 px.
- A fake screenshot of a real site.
- Shots longer than 4 s without a new element appearing.
