# Paper atlas (2d)

History and places in cut paper: parchment maps, red routes, dated labels and polaroid evidence.

Best for: history, geography, how a thing spread; calm documentary pacing.

## Rules
- Parchment background, crimson routes and stamps, ink-brown text.
- A map or a timeline in most shots; routes draw themselves as the place is named.
- Dates are labels (mono), places are labels (serif italic).
- Polaroids and case cards for real photos and documents, with sources named.
- Slow, steady: 2.5 to 4.5 s shots.

## Defaults
Captions `phrase`, shots 2.5-4.5 s, music `bed`.
Camera: No camera; slow zoom on maps.
Type: Serif italic for places, mono for dates.
Transitions: fade, cut, wipe
Sound: calm bed, paper slide whooshes, stamp on dates

## Components
RouteMap, Timeline, EvidenceCard, ProofCard, TitleCard, Stamp, BigStat

## Pitfalls
- Modern UI mixed into a paper look.
- Unsourced dates.
- Too many labels crossing the route.
