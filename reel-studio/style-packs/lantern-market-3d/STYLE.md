# Lantern Market 3D

Lantern-lit clay night market: a noodle stall, warm lanterns, a friendly cook (3D look made in Style Studio, "Lantern clay + queue in frame")

A 3D look as data (`ink_look` in style.json), made in Style Studio, look **Lantern clay + queue in frame** (the queue at x=3.3 was outside the portrait frame: moved onto the camera axis as a leading line). Approved with: "TEST RUN: the user asked the agent to test a new inkbeat 3D look end to end and show the storyboard; not a real client pick".

Render it: `node tools/ink/scaffold_plan.mjs <project>` reads `ink_look`; change it with `style_studio.mjs refine`. Preview: preview/look.jpg.
