# Reference styles: measured DNA (from 15 reference videos)

Frames and numbers read by eye and ffmpeg. Audio measured, not heard. All references are 720x1280, 30 fps unless noted. Copy the technique, never the characters, brands or text. Every pack ships an `upgrade.json` (keep / improve / change).

Common to all nine: continuous music bed, integrated loudness about -14 LUFS (LRA 1.2-3.2), no dead air under the voice, captions of 1-3 words timed to speech onset, one accent colour per scene, the camera moves in most shots.

## A1 Neon Dashboard (V3, V7)
- Dark UI card in the upper 60 percent, flat memoji-style avatar bottom-right (head top at 72 percent of frame height).
- bg `#0D0C12`, lime `#C8F848`, dim text `#8A8D95`, panel `#141C1F`.
- Caption y about 1208 of 1920, medium geometric sans. The spoken word sits in a lime rounded box with four corner handle dots (Figma selection look); inactive words dim; chunk resets every 3-5 words.
- Set pieces change state each beat (moment wheel, route map, flip calendar, node graph, checklist, counters). 0-2 hard cuts; change happens by animating cards.
- Avatar pose change about every 1.2 s: wave, point, open palm, chin, shrug, thumbs-up.

## A2 Editorial Cream/Red (V8, V6)
- bg `#E9EAE2` / `#F0EFEA`, ink `#131311`, red `#E03810`; V8 switches bg per beat (black, green `#132016`, orange-red `#E63816`).
- Mono eyebrow `// 04 - fully local` at y 141, headline 75-80 px in 2 lines, words appear only when spoken, accent word red italic serif.
- Stippled ordered-dither shadows, 7 px dot paper texture, halftone dot-field backgrounds on colour beats.
- Chrome: black numbered step badge with 5 dots, scribbles, `ENTER` keycap, `SKILL` pill. Beat 0.9-2.4 s.

## B Voxel Crate Narrator (V9, V4, V1)
- Orange matte crate pal (`^ ^` eyes, nub limbs) beside a voice character; one accent colour per scene; heavy depth-of-field blur.
- V9 starfield `#151B5C` to `#0D113C`, locked camera, 0 cuts; V4 faceted presenter with round glasses and crate crew on a wooden desk, dark caption plate with `YOU` / `CLAUDE` speaker tag, segmented progress bar; V1 light-grey trading floor, 3x5 monitor wall, wizard-hat minions, glass columns.
- Caption at about 68 percent height with one yellow keyword; step chip with progress ticks; end card lavender to peach.

## C Low-poly 3D Presenter + mascots (V2)
- Chibi presenter (head about 40 percent of torso), flat-shaded facets, big round glasses, dot eyes, brows do the acting. Mascots: one silhouette plus one glowing detail each.
- Night office, navy window, big city bokeh, desk, code-bar monitor. Shot/reverse-shot between face close-up and mascot over a blurred foreground sleeve; opens and closes on a wide; gag escalates.
- Caption: full sentences, white, dark plate at top. 25 cuts in 57 s.

## D Roblox (V10, NotAllowedToSpeak)
- Two sub-types. World-tour (V10): world is the subject, avatars small and rigid, saturated hard-sun sky, climbs by altitude (shacks, towers, mansion and pool, clouds, space), camera moves every shot (worm's-eye tilt-up, top-down drop, dutch), one yellow stroked word at about 64 percent height.
- Character-story (D2): oversized box arms, decal faces (huge eyes, toothy scream, mustache, freckles), signs as props, lens wide enough that a fist fills the frame. Captions 2 lines, uppercase emphasis word coloured by meaning (cyan names, red emotion, magenta family, green strange, orange action).
- Grade modes switched by cut: natural day, night violet-cyan, red monochrome, desaturated B&W with grain. 17 cuts in 43 s; one 7 s take. Loudness -12.2 LUFS (clips): keep a limiter.

## E Face-cam + overlay (V5)
- 21 hard cuts in 53 s, alternating face punch-in crops; face never leaves for more than 3 s. Audio -14.1 LUFS, music 6.2 dB under voice, no SFX.
- Card: x 40-680, y 155-540 at 720x1280, black 6 px bezel, 6 px section-colour border, radius 30, soft shadow, real screenshot inside. White pill chip `n/6 Label` with a section-colour dot. Face sheet from y 560 with 50 px rounded top corners on cream.
- Captions 1-3 words, white heavy sans about 48 px over the face, black over cream; one keyword per sentence in bold italic serif caps. Card-only beat grows to y 195-820.

## F 2D flat stickman in painterly PBR 3D world (V11, Railroads)
- Flat white egg head, uniform thick black outline, dot eyes, discrete expression sprites, flat colour-block costume; costume or hat carries character identity.
- Dense lit set per beat (new set about every 2.7 s), three depth layers (blurred foreground object, character, hazy far layer), PBR gold, stone, gravel, rail with emissive stripe, god rays, bloom, grain, steam puffs. Key prop recurs in about 70 percent of shots. Signage and newspapers carry facts inside the world.
- Camera never static: push, pull, truck, rack focus, macro, POV, dutch, plunge. Captions 1-2 lowercase words, white heavy sans, thick black stroke, y about 70 percent. Hard cuts rare (2 in 65 s); dissolves for time jumps.

## G Neon Stage Cube Duo (AQN0, AQOQ)
- Orange cube (doer) and pink cube with bow (asker): rounded gloss cubes, glowing square eyes, mitten arms, boots, idle bob, thought bubbles with emoji.
- Dark violet stage, glossy floor with orange grid, three spotlight cones, city bokeh; variant B retro-wave sun with scanlines and palms.
- Glass title plate with mono kicker `SKILLWARS.AI - SCENE nn` and 2-4 word uppercase title with glitch scramble; caption pill at y about 1210-1230 with orange keyword; exactly one set piece per beat; CTA keyword plate. About 3.6 cuts per 10 s, most changes by camera orbit/push.

## H 2D Sketchy Coloured Stickman (3 clips, 10-12 s)
- Clean uniform black outline 4-6 px (no wobble), white or skin round head with one-step warm rim shade, noodle limbs, mitten hands, flat colour-block clothes, big eyes with off-axis pupils, thick brows, many discrete mouth shapes; extremes on punchlines.
- Flat painted sets with the same outline weight and one saturated key colour (teal kitchen, railway platform, purple city). One set per video; wide establishing then tight face punch-in; shot/reverse-shot, cut lands on the line, about 1 cut per 2 s.
- Variants: H1 9:16 cinematic cartoon, one-word white stroked caption at y about 895 of 1280; H2 1:1 comic strip, yellow `#FFE500` title bar and white oval speech bubbles, slow continuous zoom.
