# Style contact sheets: review and fixes

The same 11-shot script (Remotion, "a video is a function of the frame number") in every style pack, with the same real screenshots.
Generated by `node tools/style_sheets.mjs <project>`; results in `output/<style-id>/` (`overview.jpg` = all 11 frames, `storyboard-1/2.jpg` = the approval sheets, `frames/`, `storyboard.json`, `critics.txt`).
Automatic critics (cinematography, storyboard) pass in every style; they measure framing rules, not looks. The notes below come from looking at the sheets.

## Defects found by looking, and fixed
| defect | where | fix |
|---|---|---|
| captions sat on the host's face | editorial-notebook, editorial-sticker | pack `safe_zone.caption_y` 1440 -> 1280 and `content_bottom` 1290 -> 1250 (captions now above the host's head) |
| proof screenshots cropped at the left or right edge, key words cut | ProofCard in paper-atlas, editorial-sticker | zoom is limited by the highlight size and its origin keeps the highlight inside the card (`evidence.tsx`); card height 900 so the caption and source line fit |
| route-map labels clipped at the right edge ("whole v") | RouteMap | labels flip to the left of a stop that is right of x=560 |
| terminal cursor drawn as an empty box (missing glyph) | Terminal in every pack | the cursor is a drawn block, not a font character |
| glass windows looked like a glitch (background showing through the lower half) | stylized-3d-presenter and other glass packs | window opacity .78 -> .93 |
| stills showed half-built cards (flow with 3 of 4 steps, timeline with one event) | storyboard sheets | `preview_shots.mjs` takes build-up components at their finished state, just before the exit fade; a shot may set `preview_at` |
| end card: small call to action, content too low | EndCard | pill 44 -> 54 px, content lifted above the caption zone |
| noir figure almost invisible | noir-3d | brighter red rim (0.9 -> 2.2) and ambient (0.14 -> 0.4) |
| the old Scene3D pack was offered next to the rebuilt ones | voxel-studio | `legacy: true`: hidden from `gallery.mjs` and `choose.mjs` (still renders; sheet kept in `output/voxel-studio/`) |
| a storyboard camera move used an unknown framing and crashed the critic | check_cinematography | reported as a blocker finding instead of crashing |

## Still open (design judgement, yours to decide)
- **noir-3d**: dark by design; a face-less figure makes front/back and speaking hard to read. Option: a minimal lit face or a stronger key on the head.
- **voxel-explainer** S02: the thick arm block covers the mouth during the cheer pose; **roblox-inspired** S01/S02 are tight (cap touches the top edge). Option: `medium-wide` for those shots in these packs.
- **voxel-studio (legacy)**: flat colours, a grey cloud blob, an extreme face crop in the close shot. Recommendation: retire it, voxel-explainer replaces it.
- **paper-atlas** S02: a two-event timeline is sparse. Option: add a third event or use a different card.
- 2D packs show an illustrated host; 3D packs do not (they have a 3D character). That is intended.
- Stills are single moments; motion (camera moves, caption animation, transitions) is judged only on the rendered video.
