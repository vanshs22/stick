Rule number one: never open the door after midnight.
I work the night shift at a small dispatch office, and nobody ever calls after 3 AM.
Tonight, the phone rang at 3:16 AM.
A woman whispered, "Please help me. Someone is standing outside my window."
I looked at my screen, and her address was empty.
Then the lights went out, and I heard slow footsteps in the hallway.
I froze, because the footsteps stopped right behind my door.
The phone rang again, and it was my own number.
I picked up, and a voice said, "Don't turn around."
So tell me, would you turn around?
