How are you so good at saving money?
Easy! I never spend a single rupee.
My friend laughed and said, "Because you are broke!"
Exactly! Zero spending, zero problems.
Then he opened his wallet, and it was completely empty.
So he did what any genius would do, and borrowed $100 from me.
Three days later, he bought a red car with my money.
I was furious, but he just smiled.
That is the smartest plan I have ever seen.
Would you lend him money again?
